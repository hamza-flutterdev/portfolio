About Me

I'm a Junior Flutter Developer at Teramob Technologies with about 1.5 years of hands-on experience shipping production-ready mobile apps. I started as an intern, and after delivering multiple applications from concept to deployment, I was promoted to a full-time role.

My work spans designing scalable databases, building offline-first experiences, and integrating AI features (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral) into real products. I also direct and debug native platform-channel modules for the pieces Dart can't reach on its own — writing core logic in Dart and C++ — while keeping the UI smooth and intuitive. On the side, I've published a Flutter package on pub.dev solo, and co-built a second one with a teammate.

Code quality, maintainability, and user experience matter a lot to me, but what I enjoy most is watching people actually use and like what I build. I work closely with cross-functional teams and take ownership of features end-to-end — from data modeling through API integration.
